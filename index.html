KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cost of installing a solar system?

The cost varies based on your energy needs and the capacity of the system. We offer free assessments to provide an accurate quote tailored to your requirements.

Are there government subsidies available?

Yes, many governments offer subsidies and tax incentives for solar installations. Our team assists you in navigating and applying for these benefits.

How long does the installation take?

A typical residential installation takes 1-3 days, while commercial projects depend on the scale. We ensure a swift and non-disruptive process.

How does Net Metering work?

Net metering allows you to send excess electricity back to the grid in exchange for credits, which you can use when your panels aren't producing enough power (e.g., at night).

What is the expected ROI?

Most of our clients see a Return on Investment within 3 to 5 years, enjoying virtually free electricity for the remaining 20+ years of the system's lifespan.
